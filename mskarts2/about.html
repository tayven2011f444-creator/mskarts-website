About MsKarts

Where dark art meets the machine

MsKarts is a one-person studio creating hand-painted 3D printed collector art. Skulls, creatures, dark butterflies, fantasy figures — every piece pushes the boundary between sculpture and raw expression.

The process starts with 3D printing highly detailed models, then each one is hand-painted using layered techniques that pull out the depth and texture the print creates. No two pieces are ever identical.

Why 3D printing?

It allows for detail and geometry that would be near-impossible to sculpt by hand — interlocking horns, lace-like wing structures, layered armor. The machine creates the form; the paint brings it to life.

Every piece ships within 3–5 days of ordering. Because it's made to order, there's no sitting inventory — what you see is what gets made for you.
