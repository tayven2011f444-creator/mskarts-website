About MsKarts

Every object starts with an idea.

MsKarts is a one-person studio making 3D printed home decor and art objects with an industrial edge. Each piece is designed, printed, and finished by hand — no outsourcing, no mass production.

The aesthetic pulls from industrial design, brutalist architecture, and the raw beauty of machines: clean geometry, honest materials, and objects that look like they were built to last.

Why 3D printing?

It lets me make things that would be impossible or impossibly expensive any other way. Lattice structures, interlocking geometry, hollow forms — 3D printing turns ideas directly into objects without tooling or molds.

Every piece ships within 3–5 days of ordering. Because it's printed to order, there's no warehouse, no waste.
